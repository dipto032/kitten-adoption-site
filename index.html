Adoption Information & Criteria

Please review our adoption guidelines below. These points serve as non-exhaustive criteria to help us select the best forever homes for the kittens:

Adoption Timeline
Post-Vaccination Transition

Kittens available after three doses of Tricat. Early handover can be discussed for experienced adopters.

Home Safety
100% Indoor & Cat-Proofed

All accessible windows and balconies must have safety netting or sturdy mesh installed prior to handover.

Pairing / Companionship
Pair Priority

Priority is given to applicants adopting a pair together or homes with an existing friendly, vaccinated cat.

Adoption Fee
Free to Loving Homes

Zero adoption fee. Adopters are expected to commit to future preventive care, rabies shots, and routine vet visits.

Commitment
Spay/Neuter Agreement

Adopters must sign a simple adoption agreement committing to mandatory sterilization at ~6 months of age.

Final Step
ID & Address Verification

Submission of valid government photo ID and proof of residence prior to the final handover visit.

💡 Need assistance? If any of these steps feel daunting—such as setting up window or balcony cat-proofing, just talk to us! We are glad to share vendor recommendations, tips, and support.
